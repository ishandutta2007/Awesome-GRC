# Awesome-GRC

## Top GRC Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Governance, Risk & Compliance, Audit Management, Policy, Controls & Regulatory Frameworks*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance)**. These systems help organizations manage risk registers, compliance frameworks, policies, controls, audits, and evidence across security, privacy, and operational risk programs.



**Examples** include AuditBoard, ServiceNow GRC, LogicGate, MetricStream, Archer IRM, OneTrust, Sprinto, Risk Cloud, and Hyperproof (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise GRC suites are mostly commercial. Practical open options include **Eramba**, **OpenGRC**, **CISO Assistant**, and **SimpleRisk**. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the enterprise gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[AuditBoard](https://www.auditboard.com/)**  

  Leading audit, risk, and compliance platform strong in SOX, internal audit workflows, and connected GRC for public companies and enterprises.



- **[ServiceNow GRC](https://www.servicenow.com/products/governance-risk-and-compliance.html)**  

  GRC capabilities on the ServiceNow platform, integrating risk, compliance, and audit with ITSM, security, and enterprise workflows.



- **[LogicGate](https://www.logicgate.com/)**  

  Modern, configurable GRC platform focused on risk and compliance workflows with strong usability for mid-market and enterprise programs.



- **[MetricStream](https://www.metricstream.com/)**  

  Enterprise GRC suite covering risk, compliance, audit, policy, and related programs for large, highly regulated organizations.



- **[Archer IRM (RSA / Archer)](https://www.archerirm.com/)**  

  Established integrated risk management platform with deep regulatory content and workflows for financial services, government, and large enterprises.



- **[OneTrust GRC](https://www.onetrust.com/)**  

  GRC capabilities within the OneTrust trust and compliance platform, often used alongside privacy, third-party risk, and ethics modules.



- **[Sprinto](https://sprinto.com/)**  

  Compliance automation platform popular with growing companies for continuous control monitoring and frameworks such as SOC 2 and ISO 27001.



- **[Risk Cloud (LogicManager / similar)](https://www.logicmanager.com/)**  

  Risk and compliance management platforms oriented toward enterprise risk programs and connected GRC processes.



- **[Hyperproof](https://hyperproof.io/)**  

  Compliance operations platform focused on evidence collection, control management, and audit readiness for modern security and compliance teams.



- **[Other GRC Cloud offerings](https://www.example.com/)**  

  Additional cloud GRC and compliance automation tools serving mid-market and specialized regulatory needs.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Eramba](https://www.eramba.org/)**  

  Long-standing open-source GRC platform covering risk, compliance, policies, incidents, and business continuity—Community Edition available for self-hosting.



- **[OpenGRC](https://github.com/LeeMangold/OpenGRC)**  

  Open-source cyber GRC web application aimed at small to mid-sized organizations managing security programs without enterprise GRC pricing.



- **[CISO Assistant](https://github.com/)**  

  Modern open-source GRC tool with broad framework support, risk management, and compliance mapping for security and GRC teams.



- **[SimpleRisk](https://www.simplerisk.com/)**  

  Open-source risk management platform focused on risk registers, assessments, and related GRC workflows (core free; commercial options available).



- **[Policy and control open frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Community collections of policy templates, control catalogs, and mapping resources (e.g., NIST, ISO) used alongside open GRC tools.



- **[Evidence and audit open trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open tools for collecting and organizing compliance evidence and audit workpapers.



- **[Risk register open spreadsheets and apps](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open risk-register applications and templates for teams not ready for a full GRC suite.



- **[Compliance checklist open projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Open checklists and workflow tools for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and similar frameworks.



- **[Integration open connectors for GRC data](https://github.com/)**  

  Scripts and connectors that pull control and asset data from cloud and security tools into open GRC systems.



- **[Documentation and runbook open repositories](https://github.com/)**  

  Community GRC playbooks and implementation guides for self-hosted deployments.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **Eramba** as a mature, full-featured open GRC option for risk, compliance, and policy management.

- Using **OpenGRC** or **CISO Assistant** for lighter-weight cyber GRC programs in mid-sized organizations.

- Combining open risk registers with commercial compliance automation (e.g., Sprinto, Hyperproof) in hybrid setups.

- Accepting that deep multi-framework enterprise content, SOX-grade audit workflows, and large-scale workflow engines still favor commercial platforms (AuditBoard, ServiceNow GRC, LogicGate, MetricStream, Archer, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on cost control, transparency, and ownership of risk and compliance data.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Maintain risks and controls in Eramba or OpenGRC → map to frameworks with open catalogs → collect evidence via integrations or manual upload → run audits and exceptions in the same system → report to leadership with open BI tools. Suitable for security and GRC teams with operational capacity. Most large enterprises adopt commercial GRC platforms for scale, content, and audit support.
